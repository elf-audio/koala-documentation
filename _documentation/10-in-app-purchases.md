In-app purchases apply to iOS and Android. On macOS, Windows and Linux, SAMURAI and MIXER are built into both the free standalone app and the paid [Koala Plugin Bundle](../13-desktop#134-koala-plugin-bundle). Everything marked [SAMURAI] or [MIXER] in this manual is already available there. The bundle also includes Koala FX and 5 sample packs with their synth toys.
