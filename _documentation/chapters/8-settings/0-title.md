Koala's settings are divided into four groups: GENERAL, AUDIO, MIDI and EXTRAS. Most settings have a small question mark icon next to the titles. Tap on them to get a short explainer on what the option does. Tap the ABOUT button at the bottom to open the about Koala website. Tap OK to close the settings window.

Some settings only exist on certain platforms. They are marked below.
