Open EXTRAS settings and tap EDIT next to QWERTY Map to view or remap any shortcut, click an action and press a key to assign it, or tap RESET to restore the default layout. You can also IMPORT and EXPORT mappings as files in EXTRAS settings.  
<span lang="zh-CN">打开 EXTRAS（其他）设置，点击 QWERTY Map 旁的 EDIT，即可查看或修改快捷键。点击某项操作后，按下要分配的键，或点击 RESET 恢复默认布局。也可在 EXTRAS 中用 IMPORT 和 EXPORT 导入和导出映射文件。</span>
