Ableton Link is available on iOS, Android and macOS.

Tap Ableton Link button to bring up the Ableton Link menu.
- **Ableton Link:** enables and disables syncing with Ableton Link.
- **In-App Notifications:** get notifications when other apps start or stop syncing through Ableton Link when this setting is on.
- **Sync Start/Stop:** sync start and stop of the sequencer when this setting is on.
- **Connected Peers:** List of other apps syncing with Koala currently and devices that can be added to Ableton Link sync.

Note: when Koala is synced to Link, external MIDI clock or an AUv3 host, the play button tells you to start and stop from the other app instead.
