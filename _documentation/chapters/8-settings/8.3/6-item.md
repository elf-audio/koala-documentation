### 8.3.6. Roland SP-404MK2 and Controller Mappings

Connect Koala to a Roland SP-404MK2 (firmware v4 or later) over USB-C to control Koala directly from the SP's pads, knobs and transport with automatic pad colours and sequence selection.

* **Banks A–D** on the SP switch between the four sample banks in Koala, while **banks E–J** remain available on the SP for sampling audio coming from Koala.
* With **Use built-in midi mappings** enabled in the MIDI settings, Koala recognises the **Roland SP-404MK2** and **Novation Launchpad Mini MK3** automatically. Tap **Configure** in the MIDI settings to inspect or customise a built-in controller mapping.

Pro tip: the SP-404MK2's Skip Back / Looper captures everything played in Koala over USB audio.

See Roland's [official SP-404MK2 × Koala Sampler setup guide](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_v4_koala_sampler/eng/index.html) for the full pad and button layout.
