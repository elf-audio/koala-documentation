**Use MIDI mapping:** enables the MIDI learn functionality. Turn this on and a button Map MIDI will appear below this option which enters the MIDI mapping mode.

In mapping mode, tap any control on the screen, then press the key or move the knob on your controller you want to use for it. The control is highlighted in the colour of the mapping scope. Tap DONE when finished. Pads, sliders, toggles, menu items, the sequence slots and perform effects can all be mapped, as can play, record, tempo, swing, quantize, undo and redo.

**Pickup:** mapped knobs only take effect once they move past the parameter's current value, preventing sudden jumps when a knob and the on-screen control disagree.

MIDI maps are saved with Koala's settings. Use the MIDI Map IMPORT and EXPORT buttons in the EXTRAS settings to move a map to another device.
