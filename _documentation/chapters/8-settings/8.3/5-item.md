Tap the Bluetooth MIDI Connect button to open the bluetooth devices screen. All known bluetooth devices are listed here, with the option to connect new devices below. Tap on any device from the list to connect it and use bluetooth MIDI device with Koala. This is available on iOS and Android.  
<span lang="zh-CN">点击 Bluetooth MIDI Connect（连接蓝牙 MIDI）打开蓝牙设备界面。界面会列出已知设备，下方可连接新设备。点击列表中的设备，即可连接并在 Koala 中使用蓝牙 MIDI。iOS 和 Android 均支持此功能。</span>
