### Where your files live
Songs, samples and settings are kept in a normal folder you can back up or sync:
* macOS: Music/Koala Sampler in your home folder
* Windows: Documents\Koala Sampler
* Linux: see the "Song folder" line in the EXTRAS settings

The exact path is shown under "Song folder" in the EXTRAS settings, and File → Open Song Folder opens it on macOS. Songs you had in older locations are moved over automatically on first launch.
