<h2 id="134-koala-plugin-bundle">13.4. Koala Plugin Bundle<br><span lang="zh-CN">13.4. Koala 插件套装</span></h2>
The Koala Plugin Bundle puts Koala Sampler and Koala FX directly inside your desktop DAW (such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Reaper or Bitwig), so songs started on your phone open right where you left off.  
<span lang="zh-CN">Koala Plugin Bundle 将 Koala Sampler 和 Koala FX 带入 Ableton Live、Logic Pro、FL Studio、Reaper 或 Bitwig 等桌面 DAW，方便你继续制作在手机上开始的工程。</span>
