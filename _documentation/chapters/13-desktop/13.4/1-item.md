**Formats & requirements**
* **macOS 11+** (Apple Silicon and Intel): Audio Unit and VST3.
* **Windows 10 version 1903+** (64-bit): VST3.
* **Linux**: CLAP and VST3.

Download from <https://koalasampler.com/plugin/>. This is separate from the standalone-only Mac App Store version. The unified installer includes the standalone app too.

**What is included**
* **Koala Sampler** as instrument and effect plugins, with SAMURAI and MIXER built in, 8 stereo output buses, and sidechain/audio input for sampling directly inside your DAW. SPLIT STEMS works inside the plugin too.
* **Koala FX**: Koala's 16 one-touch perform effects as a dedicated DAW effect plugin, with 8 snapshot scenes, an 8-way X-Y pad and full host parameter automation.
* **5 Sample Packs + 5 Synth Toys** with over 500 royalty-free samples: *Analog Astronaut* by Goldbaby (VALIS), *Cirrus Cuts* by Low Key (HOLLY), *Soon Come Dub* by Soon Come (DUB-SIREN), *Solar Studies* by London Rain (RIKER), and *Ese-O's* by Ese O (DECKARD).

**Trial & licensing**
Try the bundle free for 7 days. A single one-off Paddle licence covers up to 3 computers, with macOS and Windows mixed freely, and includes free updates. Buy, activate or deauthorize machines from **Licence** in Settings → EXTRAS, or from your online account.

**Using Koala in a DAW**
* **Transport & clock**: Koala follows your DAW's tempo and playhead when **Host clock sync** is on in AUDIO settings.
* **Keyboard shortcuts**: switch on **Enable key events** in EXTRAS settings to let Koala's plugin window capture computer keyboard shortcuts inside your DAW.
* **Drag and drop**: drag audio clips into Koala's pads or drag rendered pads straight onto DAW tracks (macOS and Windows).
* **Window sizing**: each plugin instance remembers its own editor window size.

Note: Koala does not host third-party plugins while running as a plugin itself. Load third-party synths and effects directly in your DAW instead.
