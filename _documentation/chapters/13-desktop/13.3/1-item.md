* **Drag in**: drop audio files from the Finder or Explorer, or clips straight from your DAW, onto a pad. Drop a .koala file to import a song, a .mid file onto a sequence slot to import MIDI, or a .kpk file to install a sample pack.
* **Drag out** (macOS): drag a pad out of the Koala window onto the desktop or into your DAW. The pad is rendered as you hear it, with its edits applied, to a WAV file.
* **Audio devices**: choose Input and Output devices in the AUDIO settings. On Windows you can also pick the Audio driver (including ASIO and WASAPI) and the Sample rate. On macOS Koala talks to CoreAudio directly.
* **MIDI**: USB and Bluetooth MIDI devices work as on mobile. Koala can send MIDI clock and notes to other apps through virtual ports such as the IAC bus on macOS.
* **Plugins**: Koala hosts plugins as effects and instruments: Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, CLAP and VST3 on Windows. No add-on needed on desktop. [Read more](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects).
