* **Drag in**: drop audio files from the Finder or Explorer, or clips straight from your DAW, onto a pad in the standalone app or the DAW plugin window. Drop a .koala file to import a song, a .mid file onto a sequence slot to import MIDI, or a .kpk file to install a sample pack.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**拖入**：将访达或文件资源管理器里的音频文件，或 DAW 中的音频片段，拖到独立应用或 DAW 插件窗口中的打击垫上。拖入 .koala 文件可导入工程，将 .mid 拖到音序槽位可导入 MIDI，拖入 .kpk 文件可安装采样包。</span>
* **Drag out** (macOS and Windows): drag a pad out of the Koala window onto the desktop/Explorer or straight into your DAW. The pad is rendered as you hear it, with its edits applied, to a WAV file.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**拖出**（macOS 和 Windows）：将打击垫从 Koala 窗口拖到桌面、文件资源管理器或直接拖入 DAW。Koala 会按当前听到的效果将打击垫渲染成 WAV 文件，并应用现有编辑。</span>
* **Audio devices**: choose Input and Output devices in the AUDIO settings. On Windows you can also pick the Audio driver (including ASIO and WASAPI) and the Sample rate. On macOS Koala talks to CoreAudio directly.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**音频设备**：在 AUDIO（音频）设置中选择输入和输出设备。Windows 还可选择音频驱动（含 ASIO 和 WASAPI）及采样率。macOS 上 Koala 直接使用 CoreAudio。</span>
* **MIDI**: USB and Bluetooth MIDI devices work as on mobile. Koala can send MIDI clock and notes to other apps through virtual ports such as the IAC bus on macOS.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**MIDI**：USB 和蓝牙 MIDI 的用法与移动端相同。Koala 可通过 macOS 的 IAC 总线等虚拟端口向其他应用发送 MIDI 时钟和音符。</span>
* **Plugins**: The standalone app hosts plugins as effects and instruments: Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, CLAP and VST3 on Windows and Linux. No add-on needed on desktop. [Read more](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects).  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**插件**：独立应用可加载效果器和乐器插件。macOS 支持 Audio Units、CLAP 和 VST3，Windows 和 Linux 支持 CLAP 和 VST3。桌面端可直接使用。[查看插件效果器](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects)。</span>
