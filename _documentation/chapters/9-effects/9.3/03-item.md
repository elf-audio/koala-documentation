### ZOOM WARP<br><span lang="zh-CN">缩放扭曲</span>
Continuously zooms, rotates and warps the background image.  
<span lang="zh-CN">持续缩放、旋转并扭曲背景图像。</span>

* SPEED: change the speed of rotation and zoom.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整旋转和缩放速度。</span>
* ZOOM: change the size on warped regions.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ZOOM（缩放）：调整扭曲区域的大小。</span>
