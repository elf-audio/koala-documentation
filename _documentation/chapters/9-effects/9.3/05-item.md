### WARPWARP<br><span lang="zh-CN">波形扭曲</span>
Warps and bends the background.  
<span lang="zh-CN">使背景图像扭曲和弯折。</span>

* AMOUNT: changes the amount of warping.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">AMOUNT（强度）：调整扭曲程度。</span>
* SPEED: changes the speed of warping.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整扭曲速度。</span>
