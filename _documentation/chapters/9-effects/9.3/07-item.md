### KALEIDODELIC<br><span lang="zh-CN">迷幻万花筒</span>
Mixture of kaleidoscope and psychedelic effects.  
<span lang="zh-CN">结合万花筒和迷幻视觉效果。</span>

  <span lang="zh-CN">NUMLOOPS（循环次数）：调整每个区块中的循环次数。</span>
