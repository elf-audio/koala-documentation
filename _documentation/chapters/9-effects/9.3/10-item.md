### MIRROR<br><span lang="zh-CN">镜像</span>
Mirrors and moves the image from the center in two halves.  
<span lang="zh-CN">以图像中心为界，将两半做镜像并移动。</span>

* SPEED: changes the speed of movement.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整移动速度。</span>
* ANGLE: changes the angle of mirroring.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ANGLE（角度）：调整镜像角度。</span>
