### SCROLL<br><span lang="zh-CN">滚动</span>
Infinite scroll of the image in one direction.  
<span lang="zh-CN">让图像朝一个方向无限滚动。</span>

* SPEED: change the speed of scrolling.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整滚动速度。</span>
* ANGLE: change the angle of scrolling.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ANGLE（角度）：调整滚动方向。</span>
