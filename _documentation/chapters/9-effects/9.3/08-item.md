### GLITCH<br><span lang="zh-CN">故障画面</span>
Computer screen glitch effect.  
<span lang="zh-CN">模拟电脑屏幕的显示故障。</span>

* FREQUENCY: change the speed of glitching.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">FREQUENCY（频率）：调整故障出现的频率。</span>
* AMOUNT: change the amount of glitches on the screen.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">AMOUNT（强度）：调整屏幕上的故障量。</span>
