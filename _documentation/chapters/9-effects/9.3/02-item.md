### RINGS<br><span lang="zh-CN">圆环</span>
Slices the image into rings rotating in different directions.  
<span lang="zh-CN">把图像切成朝不同方向旋转的圆环。</span>

* SPEED: change the speed of rotation.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整旋转速度。</span>
* ZOOM: change the ring’s size.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ZOOM（缩放）：调整圆环大小。</span>
