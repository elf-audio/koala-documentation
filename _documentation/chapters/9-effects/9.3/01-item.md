### BLINDS<br><span lang="zh-CN">百叶窗</span>
Slices the background into rotating lines creating an effect of window blinds.  
<span lang="zh-CN">把背景切成旋转的条带，形成百叶窗效果。</span>

* SPEED: change the speed of rotation.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整旋转速度。</span>
* ZOOM: change the size of each blind.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ZOOM（缩放）：调整各条带的大小。</span>
