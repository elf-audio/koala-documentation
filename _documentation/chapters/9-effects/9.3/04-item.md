### ZOOM<br><span lang="zh-CN">缩放</span>
Perpetually zooms the image from the center.  
<span lang="zh-CN">以图像中心为基点持续缩放。</span>

* SPEED: changes the speed of the zoom effect.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPEED（速度）：调整缩放速度。</span>
