### WARBLE<br><span lang="zh-CN">音高抖晃</span>
Emulates pitch wobble from a turntable or cassette.  
<span lang="zh-CN">模拟唱盘或磁带造成的音高抖动。</span>
* VINYL: hold to pause audio  
  <span lang="zh-CN">VINYL（黑胶）：按住可暂停音频。</span>
* AMOUNT: 0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">AMOUNT（效果量）：0–100%。</span>
* WOW/FLUTTER: 0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">WOW/FLUTTER（慢速／快速抖晃）：0–100%。</span>
* WOW SPEED: 0.05–5.0 Hz  
  <span lang="zh-CN">WOW SPEED（慢速抖晃速率）：0.05–5.0 Hz。</span>
* FLUT SPEED: 5.0–25 Hz  
  <span lang="zh-CN">FLUT SPEED（快速抖晃速率）：5.0–25 Hz。</span>
