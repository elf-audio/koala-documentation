### PLATE REVERB<br><span lang="zh-CN">板式混响</span>
Plate reverb with a bright, dense tail.  
<span lang="zh-CN">板式混响，尾音明亮而密集。</span>
* DRY/WET: 0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">DRY/WET（干湿比）：0–100%。</span>
* TIME: 0.1–30 s decay  
  <span lang="zh-CN">TIME（时间）：衰减时间 0.1 至 30 秒。</span>
* PRE-DELAY: 0–250 ms  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PRE-DELAY（预延迟）：0–250 ms。</span>
* BRIGHTNESS: 0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">BRIGHTNESS（亮度）：0–100%。</span>
* SIZE: 2–30 m  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SIZE（尺寸）：2–30 m。</span>
