### TEST TONE<br><span lang="zh-CN">测试音</span>
Signal generator for checking your setup or as a sound source for the VOCODER and SIDECHAIN.  
<span lang="zh-CN">用于检查设备设置的信号发生器，也可为 VOCODER 和 SIDECHAIN 提供声源。</span>
* PITCH: 20 Hz to 20 kHz  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PITCH（音高）：20 Hz 至 20 kHz。</span>
* TYPE: Sine, Square, Triangle, Saw, Ramp, Rand, Noise  
  <span lang="zh-CN">TYPE（波形类型）：正弦波、方波、三角波、锯齿波、斜坡波、随机或噪声。</span>
* GAIN: -96–3 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">GAIN（增益）：-96–3 dB。</span>
