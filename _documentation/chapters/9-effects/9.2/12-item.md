### LIMITER<br><span lang="zh-CN">限制器</span>
Lookahead limiter for mastering.  
<span lang="zh-CN">用于母带处理的前瞻限制器。</span>

* GAIN: -18.0–18.0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">GAIN（增益）：-18.0–18.0 dB。</span>
* ATTACK: 1.5–6.0 ms  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ATTACK（起音）：1.5–6.0 ms。</span>
* RELEASE: 60–1000 ms  
  <span lang="zh-CN">RELEASE（释音）：60–1000 ms。</span>
