### METER<br><span lang="zh-CN">测量表</span>
Various time and frequency domain information.  
<span lang="zh-CN">提供多种时域和频域测量信息。</span>

* VU: VU meters and short term/momentary/integrated LUFS  
  <span lang="zh-CN">VU（电平表）：VU 电平表，以及短时、瞬时和综合 LUFS。</span>
* SPECTRUM: visual spectrometer  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPECTRUM（频谱）：可视化频谱分析仪。</span>
* SCOPE: zoomable oscilloscope  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SCOPE（示波器）：可缩放示波器。</span>
* The METER shows peak, RMS and LUFS down to -90 dB and can be placed anywhere in the chain to inspect the signal at that point.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">METER 可显示低至 −90 dB 的峰值、RMS 和 LUFS，可放在效果链任意位置测量该处的信号。</span>
