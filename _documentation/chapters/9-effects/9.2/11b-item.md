### ISOLATOR<br><span lang="zh-CN">三段隔离均衡器</span>
DJ style three band isolator. Pull any band down to silence or push it to full.  
<span lang="zh-CN">DJ 风格的三段隔离均衡器。每段可从完全静音调到满电平。</span>
* LO, MID, HI: 0–100% level per band, with CUT at the bottom  
  <span lang="zh-CN">LO, MID, HI（低、中、高频）：各频段电平为 0–100%，拉到底为 CUT（静音）。</span>
* LO CUT: 40 Hz to 16 kHz crossover between LO and MID  
  <span lang="zh-CN">LO CUT（低切）：40 Hz 至 16 kHz，设置低频与中频之间的分频点。</span>
* HI CUT: 40 Hz to 16 kHz crossover between MID and HI  
  <span lang="zh-CN">HI CUT（高切）：40 Hz 至 16 kHz，设置中频与高频之间的分频点。</span>
