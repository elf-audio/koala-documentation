### STEREOIZER<br><span lang="zh-CN">立体声扩展</span>
Turns a mono signal into a wide stereo one while keeping the low end centred.  
<span lang="zh-CN">将单声道信号拓宽为立体声，同时让低频保持在中央。</span>
* SPREAD: 0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPREAD（扩展量）：0–100%。</span>
* LOW CUT: 90–500 Hz, frequencies below this stay mono  
  <span lang="zh-CN">LOW CUT（低切）：90 至 500 Hz，低于该频率的声音保持单声道。</span>
