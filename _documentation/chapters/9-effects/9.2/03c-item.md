### CLIPPER<br><span lang="zh-CN">硬削波器</span>
Hard clipper for adding loudness and edge, or as a brick wall before the LIMITER.  
<span lang="zh-CN">用硬削波提高响度和锐度，也可放在限制器前用作砖墙式削波。</span>
* IN: -36–36 dB input gain  
  <span lang="zh-CN">IN（输入增益）：−36 至 +36 dB。</span>
* THRESH: -45–0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">THRESH（阈值）：-45–0 dB。</span>
* OUT: -36–0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">OUT（输出增益）：-36–0 dB。</span>
* HQ: oversampling on/off for cleaner clipping at the cost of CPU  
  <span lang="zh-CN">HQ（高质量）：开启过采样可使削波更干净，但会增加 CPU 开销。</span>
