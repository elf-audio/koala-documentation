### UTILITY<br><span lang="zh-CN">实用工具</span>
Basic utilities and helpers for mixing.  
<span lang="zh-CN">用于混音的基础实用工具。</span>

* CHANNEL: Stereo, Left, Right, L+R Mono  
  <span lang="zh-CN">CHANNEL（声道）：立体声、左声道、右声道或左右混合单声道。</span>
* GAIN: -18.0–18.0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">GAIN（增益）：-18.0–18.0 dB。</span>
* FLIP PHASE: on/off  
  <span lang="zh-CN">FLIP PHASE（反相）：开启／关闭。</span>
