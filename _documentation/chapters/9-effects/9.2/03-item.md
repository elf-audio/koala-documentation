### BITCOOKER<br><span lang="zh-CN">数字降质</span>
Bit reduction and sample rate reduction with variable jitter.  
<span lang="zh-CN">降低位深和采样率，并可调节抖动量。</span>

* SAMPLERATE: 800 Hz to 44.000 kHz  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SAMPLERATE（采样率）：800 Hz 至 44.000 kHz。</span>
* BIT DEPTH: 3–24 bit  
  <span lang="zh-CN">BIT DEPTH（位深）：3–24 位。</span>
* JITTER: 0.00–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">JITTER（抖动）：0.00–100%。</span>
* MIX: 0.00–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MIX（混合量）：0.00–100%。</span>
