## 9.2. Mixer Effects

The effects below are part of the MIXER, an in-app purchase on iOS and Android and built into the desktop versions. You can also host third party plugins in the same slots. [Read more](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects) about plugin effects.

Mixer effect settings can be saved and loaded as presets, automated in sequences, and MIDI-mapped per song.

On desktop, the 16 perform effects are also available as a dedicated DAW effect plugin, **Koala FX**, in the [Koala Plugin Bundle](../13-desktop#134-koala-plugin-bundle).
