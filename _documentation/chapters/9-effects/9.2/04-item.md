### COMPRESSOR<br><span lang="zh-CN">压缩器</span>
Compresses the loudness of a signal to make it more uniform in loudness. Can also modify transients.  
<span lang="zh-CN">压缩信号的动态，使响度更均匀，也可改变瞬态。</span>

* MAKEUP: on/off  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MAKEUP（补偿增益）：开启／关闭。</span>
* KNEE/GRAPH: switch between knee indicator and spectrogram  
  <span lang="zh-CN">KNEE/GRAPH（拐点／图表）：在拐点指示器和频谱图之间切换。</span>
* THRESHOLD: -42–0.00 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">THRESHOLD（阈值）：-42–0.00 dB。</span>
* RATIO: 1.0–100  
  <span lang="zh-CN">RATIO（压缩比）：1.0–100。</span>
* ATTACK: 0.010–30 ms  
  <span lang="zh-CN">ATTACK（起音）：0.010–30 ms。</span>
* RELEASE: 10.0–1200 ms  
  <span lang="zh-CN">RELEASE（释音）：10.0–1200 ms。</span>
