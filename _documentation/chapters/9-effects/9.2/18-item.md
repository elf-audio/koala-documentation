### PITCH SHIFT<br><span lang="zh-CN">变调器</span>
Frequency domain pitch shifter.  
<span lang="zh-CN">在频域处理的变调效果。</span>

* PITCH: -12 to +12 semitones  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PITCH（音高）：−12 至 +12 个半音。</span>
* BLEND: 0.0–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">BLEND（混合量）：0.0–100%。</span>
* QUALITY: Low, Medium, High  
  <span lang="zh-CN">QUALITY（质量）：低、中、高。</span>
* STEREO: on/off  
  <span lang="zh-CN">STEREO（立体声）：开启／关闭。</span>
