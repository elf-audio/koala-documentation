### DELAY
Filterable, syncable delay.

* DELAY: 0 ms to 2.50 sec / 1/64–8 bars, set independently for LEFT and RIGHT
* STEREO: links left and right delay times on/off
* TEMPO SYNC: on/off
* FREQ: 100 Hz to 8.00 kHz
* WIDTH: 1.0–20
* FEEDBACK: 0.00–100%
* MIX: 0.00–100%
