### DRIVE<br><span lang="zh-CN">驱动失真</span>
Basic waveshaping distortion.  
<span lang="zh-CN">基础的波形整形失真。</span>

* DRIVE: 0.000–36 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">DRIVE（驱动）：0.000–36 dB。</span>
* MIX: 0.00–100%  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MIX（混合量）：0.00–100%。</span>
* OUT: -90–0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">OUT（输出增益）：-90–0 dB。</span>
* HQ: oversampling on/off for less aliasing on heavy drive  
  <span lang="zh-CN">HQ（高质量）：开启过采样可减少强驱动时的混叠。</span>
