<h2 id="59-automation-samurai">5.9. Automation [SAMURAI]<br><span lang="zh-CN">5.9. 自动化 [SAMURAI]</span></h2>
Knob and slider movements can be recorded into a sequence and edited afterwards, so your perform effects, mixer and sample parameters change over time by themselves.  
<span lang="zh-CN">可以将旋钮和滑杆的动作录入音序，并在录制后编辑。这样，演奏效果、混音器和采样参数就能随时间自动变化。</span>
