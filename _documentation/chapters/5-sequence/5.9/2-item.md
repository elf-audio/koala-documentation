### Editing automation
Double tap a sequence to open the grid, then tap the AUTOMATION button to show the automation lane. Tap CHOOSE to pick which parameter to view: the perform effects and, with the MIXER add-on, every channel's volume and effect parameters. In keyboard mode the list shows the pad's parameters, with PITCHBEND selected by default.

Three tools edit the lane:
* Pencil: draw the curve freehand.
* STEP: draw stepped values that snap to the grid.
* EDIT: drag existing points around.

The trash button clears the automation of the chosen parameter.
