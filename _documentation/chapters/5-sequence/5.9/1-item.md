### Recording automation
Press the record button on the sequencer and move a control while the sequence plays: a perform effect slider, a mixer fader or a bus effect knob. The movement is recorded into the current sequence and plays back every time the sequence loops. Sequence slots with automation show a small automation mark.

When a pad is in keyboard mode, recording captures that pad's own parameters instead: pitch bend, volume, pan, pitch, tone, attack, release, EQ and synth parameters.

Perform effects spring back to off when you release them, just like when you play them live. Other parameters stay where you left them.
