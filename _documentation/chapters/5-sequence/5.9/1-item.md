### Recording automation
Press the record button on the sequencer and move a control while the sequence plays. The movement is recorded into the current sequence and plays back every time the sequence loops. Sequence slots with automation show a small automation mark.

You can automate perform effects, mixer channel volumes and bus effect knobs. In keyboard mode you can also record the pad's own parameters: pitch bend, volume, pan, pitch, tone, attack, release, EQ, Chopper DECAY and Quokka synth parameters. Third-party plugin instrument and plugin effect parameters can be automated too.

Perform effects spring back to off when you release them, just like when you play them live. Other parameters stay where you left them.
