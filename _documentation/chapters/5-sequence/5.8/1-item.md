PEN: enables adding and removing notes in the grid view  
<span lang="zh-CN">PEN（画笔）：在网格中添加或删除音符。</span>

VEL: enables changing the velocity of the notes. Tap and slide up/down on notes to change its velocity. This also works with selections.  
<span lang="zh-CN">VEL（力度）：按住音符并上下滑动可调整力度，也可批量调整选中的音符。</span>

DICE: enables randomization of the notes. Tap and slide up/down on notes to change their chance of being played. This also works with selections.  
<span lang="zh-CN">DICE（随机）：按住音符并上下滑动可改变它被播放的概率，也可批量调整选中的音符。</span>

SNAP: enables snapping to grid when placing and moving notes.  
<span lang="zh-CN">SNAP（吸附）：放置和移动音符时将其对齐到网格。</span>

UNDO: each tap undos one previous step  
<span lang="zh-CN">UNDO（撤销）：每点一次撤销一步。</span>

REDO: each tap redos one undone step  
<span lang="zh-CN">REDO（重做）：每点一次重做一步已撤销的操作。</span>

PASTE: pastes notes copied with COPY at the playhead position.  
<span lang="zh-CN">PASTE（粘贴）：将通过 COPY 复制的音符粘贴到播放头位置。</span>

AUTOMATION: shows the automation lane below the notes. [Read more](#59-automation-samurai)  
<span lang="zh-CN">AUTOMATION（自动化）：在音符下方显示自动化轨道。[了解更多](#59-automation-samurai)。</span>

Pro tip: randomization is a great way to create ever changing sequences. Try this on your drums or melodic notes.  
<span lang="zh-CN">技巧：可以给鼓点或旋律音符设定不同的播放概率，让每次循环都略有变化。</span>
