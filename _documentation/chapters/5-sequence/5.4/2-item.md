Drag a recorded sequence over another sequence to bring up the pop-up.  
<span lang="zh-CN">将已录制的音序拖到另一条音序上，即可打开弹出菜单。</span>

MIX: mixes both sequences together. The length is extended to the longest of the both.  
<span lang="zh-CN">MIX（混合）：将两条音序合在一起，长度以较长的一条为准。</span>

ADD TO END: adds the sequence you’re dragging at the end of the other sequence. Length of the new sequence is extended by the length of the sequence you are dragging. Parameter automation from the appended sequence is copied across too.  
<span lang="zh-CN">ADD TO END（追加到末尾）：将拖动的音序接到另一条音序之后。新音序会增加所追加音序的长度，追加音序中的参数自动化也会一并复制。</span>

SWAP: swaps the two sequences.  
<span lang="zh-CN">SWAP（交换）：交换两条音序。</span>

CANCEL: cancels mixing operation.  
<span lang="zh-CN">CANCEL（取消）：取消混合操作。</span>

Pro tip: tap and hold a sequence slot to open its menu, where you can give the sequence a COLOR.  
<span lang="zh-CN">技巧：按住音序槽位可打开菜单，通过 COLOR（颜色）为音序设置颜色。</span>
