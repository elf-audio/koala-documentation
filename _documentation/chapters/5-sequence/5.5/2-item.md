Tap the BARS button to bring up the menu.  
<span lang="zh-CN">点击 BARS（小节数）按钮打开菜单。</span>

DOUBLE UP: doubles the length of the selected sequence, duplicating all notes and settings.  
<span lang="zh-CN">DOUBLE UP（长度加倍）：将选中音序的长度翻倍，并复制所有音符和设置。</span>

* **+** adds one bar  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**＋** 增加一小节。</span>
* **-** removes one bar  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**−** 减少一小节。</span>
