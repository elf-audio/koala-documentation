KEYBOARD MODE: switches to one of the available keyboard modes and allows you to play pads chromatically, in a predefined scale or as note repeats. Tap on the keyboard button, the on the pad you want to play in keyboard mode.

ARP: appears when the arpeggiator is switched on in the keyboard scale menu. Tap it to open the arpeggiator settings. [Read more](#5610-arpeggiator)

UNDO and REDO: step back and forward through everything you did to the song, not only in the sequencer. Edits to samples, mixer settings and effects can be undone too. On desktop use Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+Z.

CLEAR: clears selected sequence. Alternatively, drag a sequence up into a trash area to clear it. Hold CLEAR and tap the KEYBOARD button to clear only the notes of the current pad from the sequence.

BANKS: the 32 sequence slots are split over four pages. Tap the arrows next to the slots, or swipe the slots sideways, to move between them.
