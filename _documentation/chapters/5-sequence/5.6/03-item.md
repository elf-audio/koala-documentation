GRID mode shows notes locked to one of the available scales:  
<span lang="zh-CN">GRID（网格）模式将音符限定在当前音阶内，可选以下音阶。</span>

* CHROMATIC: all 12 notes per octave are available  
  <span lang="zh-CN">CHROMATIC（半音阶）：每个八度包含全部 12 个音。</span>
* PENTATONIC: 5 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PENTATONIC（五声音阶）：每个八度 5 个音。</span>
* MAJOR: 7 notes of the major scale per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MAJOR（大调音阶）：每个八度 7 个音。</span>
* NATURAL MINOR: 7 notes of the natural minor scale per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">NATURAL MINOR（自然小调音阶）：每个八度 7 个音。</span>
* HARMONIC MINOR: 7 notes of the harmonic minor scale per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">HARMONIC MINOR（和声小调音阶）：每个八度 7 个音。</span>
* WHOLE TONE: 6 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">WHOLE TONE（全音音阶）：每个八度 6 个音。</span>
* DIMINISHED: 8 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">DIMINISHED（减音阶）：每个八度 8 个音。</span>
* HEXATONIC: 6 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">HEXATONIC（六声音阶）：每个八度 6 个音。</span>
* JAPANESE: 5 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">JAPANESE（日本音阶）：每个八度 5 个音。</span>
* PELOG: 5 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PELOG（佩洛格音阶）：每个八度 5 个音。</span>
* YING ZHONG: 5 notes per octave  
  <span lang="zh-CN">YING ZHONG（应钟音阶）：每个八度 5 个音。</span>

Use the selector to change scales. Tap the cog on the grid to open GRID SETTINGS: set how many rows and columns to show, switch "Vertical moves in 4ths" on to lay the grid out in fourths like a bass guitar, and "Show note names" to label every cell.  
<span lang="zh-CN">用选择器切换音阶。点击网格上的齿轮打开 GRID SETTINGS（网格设置），可以设定显示的行数和列数。打开 Vertical moves in 4ths（纵向按四度排列），可像贝斯指板一样按四度排列。打开 Show note names（显示音名），可在每个格子上标注音名。</span>

Note: tap several areas to play chords of up to 5 voices.  
<span lang="zh-CN">注意：同时点击多个格子可演奏最多五个声部的和弦。</span>

Pro tip: resample a chord to a pad, then use that pad in grid keyboard mode again to play chords that are always in scale.  
<span lang="zh-CN">技巧：把和弦重采样到打击垫，再用网格键盘模式演奏该打击垫，可按所选音阶的音级触发移调后的和弦。</span>
