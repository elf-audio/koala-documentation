### 5.6.10. Arpeggiator
Open the scale dropdown while in keyboard mode and tick ARPEGGIATOR. Held notes are now played one at a time in tempo, and an ARP button appears in the sequence controls. Tap ARP to open the settings:

* RATE: 4, 4T, 8, 8T, 16, 16T, 32 or 64 steps per bar. Tick FREE in the mode menu to use a continuous RATE slider instead, which is not locked to the grid so you can glide the speed.
* Mode: UP, DOWN, RANDOM, RAND OTHER (random, but never the same note twice in a row), ORDER (in the order you pressed the notes) or REPEAT (retriggers all held notes together).

The arpeggiator works in every keyboard mode, with MIDI input and when recording into a sequence.
