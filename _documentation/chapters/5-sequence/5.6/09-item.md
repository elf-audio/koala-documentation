{% flexible_image link="sequence-note-repeat-advanced-menu.png" alt="Note repeat advanced dropdown" %}

Tap on the dropdown to select between PITCH, VEL or OFF modes.  
<span lang="zh-CN">点击下拉菜单，选择 PITCH（音高）、VEL（力度）或 OFF（关闭）。</span>
* PITCH changes the pitch of the pad by sliding up or down.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">PITCH：上下滑动可改变打击垫的音高。</span>
* VEL changes the velocity by sliding up or down.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">VEL：上下滑动可改变力度。</span>
* OFF disables additional controls.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">OFF：关闭附加控件。</span>

{% flexible_image link="sequence-note-repeat-pitch-scale-menu.png" alt="Note repeat pitch scale menu" %}

Tap the cog icon in PITCH mode to open PITCH SCALE, where you can choose the scale and set how far the pitch slide reaches (1, 2 or 3 OCTAVES).  
<span lang="zh-CN">在 PITCH 模式下点击齿轮，打开 PITCH SCALE（音阶设置）。可以选择音阶，并将滑动变调的范围设为 1、2 或 3 个八度。</span>
