* DELETE: deletes selected notes  
  <span lang="zh-CN">DELETE（删除）：删除所选音符。</span>
* COPY: copies selected notes to the buffer  
  <span lang="zh-CN">COPY（复制）：将所选音符复制到剪贴板。</span>
* SNAP: snaps selected notes by the snap setting.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SNAP（吸附）：按当前吸附设置对齐所选音符。</span>
