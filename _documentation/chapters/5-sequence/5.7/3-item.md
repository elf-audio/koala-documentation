Tapping on notes that were recorded using the keyboard mode opens the piano roll view. It automatically scrolls to bring existing notes into view. Tap the keyboard icon to show a keyboard at the side of the piano roll so you can audition notes while editing.

On desktop, scroll the mouse wheel over the key column to zoom vertically, or over the timeline bar to zoom horizontally.

Note: the piano roll is available as in-app purchase SAMURAI. [Read more](../10-in-app-purchases#101-samurai) about SAMURAI.
