<h3 id="1013-autochop-and-chopper">10.1.3. Autochop and Chopper<br><span lang="zh-CN">10.1.3. 自动切片与 Chopper</span></h3>
Slices your beats automatically, auto (threshold based), manual and the classic MPC like lazy chop. The CHOPPER instrument plays slices from a single pad with note, velocity, random and next trigger modes. [Read more](../4-sample#418-chopper-samurai).  
<span lang="zh-CN">可按阈值自动切片，也可手动切片，或使用经典的 MPC 式 LAZY 切片。CHOPPER 乐器可从一个打击垫上播放多个切片，支持按音符、力度、随机或顺序触发。[了解 Chopper](../4-sample#418-chopper-samurai)。</span>
