### 10.1.1. Timestretch
Time stretching allows locking the playback of the sample to a specific set of beats and bars synced to the tempo of your song. 5 algorithms to stretch your samples so they fit perfectly with your song's BPM, and change pitch independently of time. MODERN sounds buttery smooth, RETRO emulates classic sampler granular timestretch, BEATS uses transients in your sample to make chops and loops to extend or shorten your sample, REPITCH changes the pitch like speeding up a record, and CYCLIC recreates retro time-stretching reminiscent of 90s jungle. [Read more](../4-sample#411-time-stretching).
