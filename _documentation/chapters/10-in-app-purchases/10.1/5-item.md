### 10.1.5. Quokka Synth, MIDI Out and Automation
QUOKKA is a powerhouse polyphonic synthesizer that lives on a pad, with presets you can save and share. MIDI-OUT pads let Koala's sequencer control external hardware. Automation records your knob and effect movements into sequences for editing in the piano roll. [Read more](../4-sample#417-quokka-synth-samurai) about Quokka, [MIDI out](../4-sample#419-midi-out-pads-samurai) and [automation](../5-sequence#59-automation-samurai).

SAMURAI also enables Pocket Operator sync out.
