Add up to 5 effects to the master channel to create a unique character to your song.

The mixer also lets you load external plugins into mixer slots and onto pads in the standalone app on every platform except Android, which has no native plugin format: AUv3 on iOS, Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, and CLAP and VST3 on Windows and Linux.
