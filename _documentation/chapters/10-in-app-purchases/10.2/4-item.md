Add up to 5 effects to the master channel to create a unique character to your song.  
<span lang="zh-CN">可在主通道载入最多 5 个效果，为整个工程塑造音色。</span>

The mixer also lets you load external plugins into mixer slots and onto pads in the standalone app on every platform except Android, which has no native plugin format: AUv3 on iOS, Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, and CLAP and VST3 on Windows and Linux. [Read more](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects).  
<span lang="zh-CN">混音器还支持在独立应用的混音器槽位和打击垫中载入外部插件。iOS 支持 AUv3，macOS 支持 Audio Units、CLAP 和 VST3，Windows 和 Linux 支持 CLAP 和 VST3。Android 缺少原生插件格式，因此不支持此功能。[查看插件效果器](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects)。</span>
