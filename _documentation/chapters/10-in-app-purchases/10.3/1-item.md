Open the SAMPLES browser and tap GET MORE SAMPLES to browse the packs. Tap a pack to hear a preview and read about it, then buy it with your app store account. The pack downloads and appears in the PACKS section of the browser, where you can load individual sounds and whole KITS, or play the pack's toy and sample it onto a pad. [Read more](../4-sample#413-samples-browser-basics-packs-kits-and-more).

Packs are tied to your app store account. On a new device, tap RESTORE in the store or "Restore purchases" in the EXTRAS settings to download them again at no cost. If you are short on space, "Sample Packs" in the EXTRAS settings lets you uninstall downloaded packs. You can always download them again.
