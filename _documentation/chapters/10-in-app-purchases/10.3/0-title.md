## 10.3. Sample Packs
Sample packs are collections of sounds, kits and sometimes a free toy, sold inside Koala on iOS and Android.

On desktop, five sample packs and all five synth toys come bundled with the [Koala Plugin Bundle](../13-desktop#134-koala-plugin-bundle). You can also drop .kpk sample pack files onto Koala to install them.
