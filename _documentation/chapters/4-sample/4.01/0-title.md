<h2 id="41-sample-sources">4.1. Sample Sources<br><span lang="zh-CN">4.1. 采样来源</span></h2>

Koala can sample from the mic, audio files, video files, audio interfaces, resampling, device system audio (SYSTEM), iOS AUv3 effect input and desktop DAW plugin input.  
<span lang="zh-CN">Koala 支持从麦克风、音频文件、视频文件、音频接口、应用内重采样、设备系统音频（SYSTEM）、iOS AUv3 效果器输入和桌面 DAW 插件输入采样。</span>
