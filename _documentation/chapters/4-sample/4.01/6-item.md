### Recording tips<br><span lang="zh-CN">录音技巧</span>
* **Record system audio (SYSTEM)**: pick SYSTEM in the input source menu to sample whatever audio your device is currently playing. Requires iOS 27+, macOS 14.2+, Android 10+ or Windows.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**录制系统音频（SYSTEM）：**在输入源菜单中选择 SYSTEM，即可采集设备当前播放的音频。需要 iOS 27 及以上、macOS 14.2 及以上、Android 10 及以上或 Windows。</span>
* **Lock the recording**: with "Hold to record" on, swipe up from the recording pad to the HOLD marker to keep recording after you lift your finger. Tap the pad again to stop.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**锁定录音：**开启 Hold to record（按住录音）后，从正在录音的打击垫向上滑到 HOLD 标记。松手后录音仍会持续，再次点击该打击垫即可停止。</span>
* **Record several pads in a row**: while a pad is recording, tap another empty pad. Recording stops on the first pad and continues on the new one without a gap. This needs the recording to be locked as above, or "Hold to record" switched off in the GENERAL settings.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**连续录入多个打击垫：**一个打击垫正在录音时，点击另一个空打击垫。录音会立即从前一个打击垫切换到新打击垫，中间没有间隙。使用此功能前，请锁定录音，或在 GENERAL（常规）设置中关闭 Hold to record。</span>
* **Loop recording**: swipe down from the recording pad to the LOOP marker, whether "Hold to record" is switched on or off. Koala waits for the next bar line (counting in with the metronome if the sequencer is stopped), records, and stops on a bar line so the result loops cleanly at your song's tempo.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**按小节录制循环：**从正在录音的打击垫下滑至 LOOP 标记。Hold to record 开启或关闭时均可使用。Koala 会从下一小节开始录音，并在小节边界结束，使录音能按当前工程速度无缝循环。音序器停止时，节拍器会先预数。</span>
* **Resample a single bus**: with the MIXER add-on the resample option becomes RESAMPLE : MAIN. Tap it to choose MAIN or BUS A to D as the source, so you can resample one channel with its effects.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**重采样单条总线：**解锁 MIXER（混音器）后，重采样选项会显示为 RESAMPLE : MAIN。点击它可选择 MAIN（主通道）或 BUS A 至 D 作为输入源，将该通道连同效果一起录下。</span>
* **Auto trim and auto normalize**: Koala trims silence from the start of a recording and brings quiet recordings up to a healthy level. Both can be switched off in the AUDIO settings.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**自动修剪与自动归一化：**Koala 会去掉录音开头的静音，并将过轻的录音提高到合适的电平。这两项功能都可以在 AUDIO（音频）设置中关闭。</span>
