### Recording tips
* **Lock the recording**: with "Hold to record" on, swipe up from the recording pad to the HOLD marker to keep recording after you lift your finger. Tap the pad again to stop.
* **Record several pads in a row**: while a pad is recording, tap another empty pad. Recording stops on the first pad and continues on the new one without a gap. This needs the recording to be locked as above, or "Hold to record" switched off in the GENERAL settings.
* **Loop recording**: swipe down from the recording pad to the LOOP marker. Koala waits for the next bar line (counting in with the metronome if the sequencer is stopped), records, and stops on a bar line so the result loops cleanly at your song's tempo.
* **Resample a single bus**: with the MIXER add-on the resample option becomes RESAMPLE : MAIN. Tap it to choose MAIN or BUS A to D as the source, so you can resample one channel with its effects.
* **Auto trim and auto normalize**: Koala trims silence from the start of a recording and brings quiet recordings up to a healthy level. Both can be switched off in the AUDIO settings.
