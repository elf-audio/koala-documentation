Use one of the 8 built-in input effects to mangle input sound during the recording. Following effects are available:  
<span lang="zh-CN">录音时可选择 8 种内置输入效果中的一种来处理音频。可用效果如下：</span>

* **More Bass:** low end boost  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**More Bass（增强低音）：**提升低频。</span>
* **More Treble:** mid range boost  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**More Treble（增强高音）：**提升中频。</span>
* **Fuzz:** classic fuzz overdrive  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**Fuzz（模糊失真）：**经典的 Fuzz 过载效果。</span>
* **Robot:** adds a robotic flavor  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**Robot（机器人）：**让声音带有机械感。</span>
* **Reverb:** basic reverb  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**Reverb（混响）：**基础混响效果。</span>
* **8ve Up:** pitches audio 1 octave up  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**8ve Up（升高八度）：**将音频升高一个八度。</span>
* **8ve Down:** pitches audio 1 octave down  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**8ve Down（降低八度）：**将音频降低一个八度。</span>
* **Bass Synth:** adds low end harmonics  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**Bass Synth（低音合成）：**增加低频谐波。</span>
