Select import audio to bring up file selector when tapping on an empty pad. Selected sample will then get loaded on the same pad.  
<span lang="zh-CN">选择导入音频后，点击空打击垫即可打开文件选择器。选中的采样会加载到刚才点击的打击垫。</span>

Supported audio formats are whatever your OS is able to play back natively with the default media player.  
<span lang="zh-CN">设备操作系统的默认媒体播放器可原生播放的音频格式均受支持。</span>

Audio files with more than two channels are automatically mixed down to stereo on import.  
<span lang="zh-CN">导入超过两个声道的音频文件时，会自动缩混为立体声。</span>
