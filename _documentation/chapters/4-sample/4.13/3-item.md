### Toys
Some sample packs include a free toy: a small playable instrument or effect that opens inside the SAMPLES browser. Toys appear in the MORE section once you own the pack that contains them. Tap a toy to open it and read what it does.

Toys do not live on pads. Instead, their audio is automatically routed to Koala's input while the toy is open, so you sample them like a microphone: record to a free pad in the normal way and whatever the toy is doing lands on the pad like any other sample.

* **DECKARD**: a turntable. Load a sample onto the deck, scratch the platter, PUNCH the sound in, use the crossfader and switch between VINYL and SPECTRAL scratching. Knobs for SPEED and PITCH.
* **HOLLY**: a retro voice synthesizer. Type in some text and Holly speaks it. Shape the voice with the PITCH, SPEED and FORMANT knobs, or play it backwards with REVERSE.
* **RIKER**: a drum synth plugged into a reverb experiment gone wrong. Tweak DELAY, DIFFUSION, SCATTER, SHIFT and FEEDBACK.
* **DUB-SIREN**: a classic sound system siren. Hold HORN to sound it, choose a MODE (POLICE, ROBOT, BEEP, SLIDE), set PITCH, modulation DEPTH and SPEED, delay AMOUNT and TIME, and switch RETRIG on to retrigger the sweep on every press.
* **VALIS**: an enigmatic preset synthesizer based on cross-modulation synthesis, with 1,099,511,627,775 presets. Tap the randomiser or type a preset number to find a new one.

Pro tip: switch on input FX while recording a toy to mangle it on the way in, just like with the mic.
