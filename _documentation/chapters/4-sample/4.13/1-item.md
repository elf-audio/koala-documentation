* **BASICS**: the built-in library of around 250 sounds that ships with Koala. Browse by category, tap a sound to preview it, then tap LOAD or drag it onto a pad.
* **PACKS**: sample packs you have bought or downloaded. Each pack has a cover, a description (tap MORE INFO) and a preview. Once a pack is installed, the BASICS section is folded into PACKS.
* **GET MORE SAMPLES** (iOS and Android only): opens the sample pack store, where you can browse and buy new packs without leaving Koala. Purchased packs download straight into the PACKS section. Use RESTORE on the store page or in the EXTRAS settings if a pack you own does not show up.
* **Songs**: your saved Koala songs. [Read more](#43-import-audio) about loading samples and banks from other songs.
* **MORE**: instruments and toys. [Read more](#417-quokka-synth-samurai) about Koala's instruments.
* **Custom locations**: any folders you added with ADD LOCATION.

Pro tip: tap the speaker icon in the browser to change the preview volume.
