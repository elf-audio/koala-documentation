### Kits
Many sample packs come with KITS: ready-made sets of 16 sounds laid out as a bank. Select a pack, choose a kit in the KIT dropdown, tap the pads to preview them and tap LOAD KIT to load the whole kit into the current bank.

Warning: loading a kit overwrites all samples in the current bank. Switch to an empty bank first if you want to keep what you have.
