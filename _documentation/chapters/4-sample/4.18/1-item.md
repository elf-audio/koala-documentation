Select a pad with a sample, open TOOLS and tap CONVERT TO CHOPPER. The waveform shows the slice markers. Drag the CHOPS bar to change the number of slices. Tap the waveform to preview slices. CONVERT TO SAMPLE turns it back into a normal pad.

* **SLICE MODE**: AUTO places slices on transients, with SENSITIVITY controlling how many it finds. EQUAL divides the sample into equal parts.
* **TRIGGER MODE**: NOTE plays one slice per key in keyboard mode or per MIDI note. VELOCITY chooses the slice from how hard you hit the pad. RANDOM picks a random slice on every hit. NEXT steps through the slices in order, one per hit.
* **ONE SHOT**: when on, a slice plays to its end on tap. When off, it plays only as long as you hold the pad or key.
* **MONO**: when on, only one slice sounds at a time and a new slice cuts off the previous one. When off, slices can overlap.
* **PLAY THRU**: when on, a slice keeps playing past the next marker to the end of the sample. When off, it stops at the next marker.
