Select a pad with a sample, open TOOLS and tap CONVERT TO CHOPPER. The waveform shows the slice markers. The toolbar includes the CHOP: selector. Drag the CHOPS bar to change the number of slices. Tap the waveform to preview slices. CONVERT TO SAMPLE turns it back into a normal pad.

* **SLICE MODE**: AUTO places slices on transients, with SENSITIVITY controlling how many it finds. EQUAL divides the sample into equal parts.
* **TRIGGER MODE**: NOTE plays one slice per key in keyboard mode or per MIDI note. VELOCITY chooses the slice from how hard you hit the pad. RANDOM picks a random slice on every hit. NEXT steps through the slices in order, one per hit.
