The TOOLS menu of a Chopper pad has two extra entries:  
<span lang="zh-CN">Chopper 打击垫的 TOOLS（工具）菜单多出两个选项。</span>

* **EXTRACT SLICES**: copies every slice to its own free pad, like AUTO-CHOP.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**EXTRACT SLICES（提取切片）**：将各切片分别复制到空打击垫，效果类似 AUTO-CHOP。</span>
* **EXTRACT PATTERN**: writes a sequence that plays the slices in their original order, so you can rearrange the chops in the piano roll.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**EXTRACT PATTERN（提取音序）**：生成一条按原顺序播放切片的音序，方便在钢琴卷帘中重新编排。</span>
