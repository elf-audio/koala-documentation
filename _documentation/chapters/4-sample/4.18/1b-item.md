* **ONE SHOT**: when on, a slice plays to its end on tap. When off, it plays only as long as you hold the pad or key.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**ONE SHOT（单次播放）**：开启后，点一下便会把切片播到末尾。关闭后，仅在按住打击垫或琴键时播放。</span>
* **MONO**: when on, only one slice sounds at a time and a new slice cuts off the previous one. When off, slices can overlap.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**MONO（单音）**：开启后，同一时间只播放一个切片，新切片会截断上一个。关闭后，切片可重叠播放。</span>
* **DECAY**: shapes the decay envelope of played slices, and can be automated in sequences.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**DECAY（衰减）**：调整切片的衰减包络，也可在音序中录制该参数的自动化。</span>
* **PLAY THRU**: when on, a slice keeps playing past the next marker to the end of the sample. When off, it stops at the next marker.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">**PLAY THRU（播到末尾）**：开启后，切片会越过下一个标记，播到原采样末尾。关闭后，在下一个标记处停止。</span>
