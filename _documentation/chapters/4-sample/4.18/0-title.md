<h2 id="418-chopper-samurai">4.18. Chopper [SAMURAI]<br><span lang="zh-CN">4.18. Chopper 切片乐器 [SAMURAI]</span></h2>
Chopper turns a single sample into a playable instrument where every note plays a different slice. It is the quick alternative to AUTO-CHOP when you do not want to use up 16 pads.  
<span lang="zh-CN">Chopper 将一个采样变成可演奏的乐器，每个音符可播放不同切片。想快速切片又想省下 16 个打击垫时，可用它代替 AUTO-CHOP（自动切片）。</span>
