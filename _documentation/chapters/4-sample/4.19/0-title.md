<h2 id="419-midi-out-pads-samurai">4.19. MIDI Out Pads [SAMURAI]<br><span lang="zh-CN">4.19. MIDI 输出打击垫 [SAMURAI]</span></h2>
A MIDI-OUT pad sends MIDI notes to external hardware or other apps instead of playing a sound, so you can sequence a drum machine or synth with Koala's sequencer.  
<span lang="zh-CN">MIDI-OUT 打击垫会把 MIDI 音符发送给外部硬件或其他应用。你可以用 Koala 的音序器编排外接鼓机或合成器。</span>
