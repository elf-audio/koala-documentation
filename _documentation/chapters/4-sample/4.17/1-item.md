To put Quokka on a pad, open the SAMPLES browser, go to the MORE section, tap QUOKKA SYNTH and drag it onto a free pad or tap LOAD. Tap EDIT with the pad selected to open the Quokka editor. Play it from the pad, from the keyboard modes or over MIDI.

Quokka's controls are spread over tabs:

* **OSC**: SHAPE blends from saw, through square, sub and noise. MORPH shapes the waveform further. OCTAVE shifts two octaves up or down. MONO switches from polyphonic to a single monophonic voice, GLIDE sets the portamento time between notes.
* **FILTER**: filter mode, CUTOFF, RES and DRIVE.
* **ENV**: the amplitude envelope with A, D, S and R.
* **MOD**: a second envelope with TIME, TARGET and DEPTH. Targets are PITCH, SHAPE, MORPH, CUTOFF and AMP.
* **LFO**: shape (sine, square, triangle, saw, ramp, random, noise), SPEED, tempo sync, TARGET, DEPTH and whether the LFO retriggers on every note.
