To put Quokka on a pad, open the SAMPLES browser, go to the MORE section and drag QUOKKA onto a pad. Tap EDIT with the pad selected to open the Quokka editor. Play it from the pad, from the keyboard modes or over MIDI.

On phones, Quokka's sections are organised across tabs OSC, FILTER, LFO, ENV and PRESETS. On tablets, OSCILLATOR, FILTER, LFO and ENVELOPE appear side by side.

* **OSC**: SHAPE blends from saw, through square, sub and noise. MORPH shapes the waveform further. OCTAVE shifts two octaves up or down. MONO switches from polyphonic to a single monophonic voice, GLIDE sets the portamento time between notes.
* **FILTER**: filter mode, CUTOFF, RES and DRIVE.
