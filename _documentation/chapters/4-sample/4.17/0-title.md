## 4.17. Quokka Synth [SAMURAI]
Pads in Koala do not have to hold samples. With the SAMURAI add-on a pad can be an instrument, and Quokka is Koala's built-in synthesizer. It is a mono or polyphonic synth with a single wave-blend oscillator, a filter, two envelopes and an LFO.
