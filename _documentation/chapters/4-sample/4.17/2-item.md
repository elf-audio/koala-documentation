### Presets
Tap PRESETS to open the preset browser. Pick a FACTORY preset or one of your own USER presets, use the arrows to step through them and tap SAVE to store the current sound under a new name. The preset menu lets you EXPORT PRESET, EXPORT ALL USER PRESETS and IMPORT PRESETS so you can move sounds between devices, DELETE a user preset or RESET TO DEFAULT.

Volume, pan and pitch of a Quokka pad work like on a sample pad, and all the synth parameters can be automated. [Read more](../5-sequence#59-automation-samurai) about automation.

Pro tip: resample a Quokka pad to turn it into a regular sample you can chop, stretch and reverse.
