* **LFO**: shape (sine, square, triangle, saw, ramp, random, noise), SPEED, tempo sync, TARGET, DEPTH and whether the LFO retriggers on every note.
* **ENV**: switches between the **AMP** envelope (A, D, S, R) and **MOD** envelope (A, D, S, R or modulation controls). MOD modulation controls are TIME, TARGET and DEPTH; targets are PITCH, SHAPE, MORPH, CUTOFF and AMP.
