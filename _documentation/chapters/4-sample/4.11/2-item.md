Tap on top of the selected area with the number of bars to open the TIMESTRETCH window. Choose how long the selection should play: 1 BEAT, 2 BEATS, 1 BAR, 2, 4, 8 or 16 BARS, or CUSTOM for any number of beats and bars. Tap PREVIEW to hear the result before closing the window.

Select one of the five stretching modes. Every mode has its distinct sound.
* MODERN: smooth, clean stretching for most material.
* RETRO: emulates the granular time stretch of classic samplers.
* BEATS: uses the transients in your sample, particularly useful for drum loops.
* REPITCH: simply changes the pitch to change the length, like speeding up a record.
* CYCLIC: recreates the cyclic time stretching reminiscent of 90s jungle.

Note: MODERN and REPITCH cannot be played chromatically. Use RETRO, BEATS or CYCLIC if you want to play a stretched pad in keyboard mode or from the piano roll. [Read more](../5-sequence#56-keyboard-modes) about keyboard mode.

Pro tip: using long durations on short selections creates interesting ambient sounds. Create your own granular sounding synth by playing stretched pads in chromatic mode.
