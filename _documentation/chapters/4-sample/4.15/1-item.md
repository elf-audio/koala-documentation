Open the TOOLS dropdown and tap COLOR to choose a colour for the selected pad. Pick from one of four palettes: KOALA, TOL, WONG or IBM. Multi-select pads with EDIT to colour several at once.  
<span lang="zh-CN">打开 TOOLS（工具）下拉菜单，点 COLOR（颜色）为所选打击垫配色。可选 KOALA、TOL、WONG 和 IBM 四种调色板。按住 EDIT（编辑）可多选打击垫并批量配色。</span>

Tap and hold a sequence slot, then tap COLOR to colour a sequence the same way.  
<span lang="zh-CN">按住音序槽位，再点击 COLOR，可用同样的方法设置音序颜色。</span>

Notes in the piano roll take the colour of their pad, so a coloured drum kit is much easier to read in the grid.  
<span lang="zh-CN">钢琴卷帘中的音符沿用对应打击垫的颜色。为鼓组配色后，就更容易在网格里辨认各种鼓的音符。</span>
