You can add any folder on your device to your favorites by tapping on the ADD LOCATION button. Tap EDIT button to remove folders from your favorites. Tap on the cloud button to open iCloud in the Files app (iOS 14 and later). On desktop there is no cloud button, use the normal file browser instead.

Tap on the multi select button in the top right corner, which enables selecting and loading multiple samples in one go.
