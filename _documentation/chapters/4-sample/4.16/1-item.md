Drag in the waveform to select a region. Pinch to zoom. The SNAP dropdown snaps your selection to FREE, 1/16, 1 BEAT, 1 BAR or 4 BARS at the song tempo. Tap PLAY to hear the selection, switch LOOP on to loop it while you work, and switch between the WAVE and SPECTRUM views.

* CUT, COPY, PASTE, DELETE: standard clipboard editing within the sample.
* MODIFY opens a menu with CROP, FADE IN, FADE OUT, INVERT, REVERSE, NORMALIZE, TRIM SILENCE and GENERATE..., applied to the selection.
* UNDO and REDO step back and forward through your edits.

Note: wave editor changes are written into the sample itself.
