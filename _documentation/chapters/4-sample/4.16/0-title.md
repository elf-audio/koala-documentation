<h2 id="416-wave-editor">4.16. Wave Editor<br><span lang="zh-CN">4.16. 波形编辑器</span></h2>
The wave editor is a destructive sample editor for cutting, pasting, fading and generating audio. Open the TOOLS dropdown and tap EDIT. On desktop you can also press Cmd/Ctrl+E.  
<span lang="zh-CN">波形编辑器可剪切、粘贴、淡化和生成音频，编辑会直接改写采样。打开 TOOLS（工具）下拉菜单，点击 EDIT（编辑）。桌面端也可以按 Cmd+E 或 Ctrl+E。</span>
