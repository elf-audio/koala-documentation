CHOKE: a per-pad setting with choke groups 1 to 6 plus SELF. Pads in the same choke group cut each other off, like open and closed hi-hats. Any pad assigned to a choke group or SELF also chokes itself when retriggered.

QUALITY: lower the sample rate and switch interpolation between HI-QUALITY, CLASSIC and RETRO per sample to dial in vintage sampler crunch and aliasing.

STRETCH [SAMURAI]: activates time stretching for the sample. [Read more](#411-time-stretching). STRETCH is only available in SAMURAI, an in-app purchase.

Pro tip: tap and hold EDIT, then select all the pads you want to tweak. Control changes will be applied to all selected pads.
