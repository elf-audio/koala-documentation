TOOLS: opens a dropdown menu with additional tools. Pro tip: drag up or down on TOOLS to open it without tapping.
* LABEL: add a custom label to the pad
* INFO: show details about the sample. [Read more](#414-sample-info)
* EDIT: open the wave editor. [Read more](#416-wave-editor)
* COLOR: choose a colour for the pad. [Read more](#415-pad-and-sequence-colours)
* CROP: trim the sample to selected area
* NORMALIZE: normalizes the sample to 0 dB
* AUTO-CHOP [SAMURAI]: divide sample into chops. [Read more](../10-in-app-purchases#101-samurai) about SAMURAI add-on
* SPLIT STEMS: separate stems into drums, vocals, bass and other instruments. [Read more](../4-sample#410-split-stems)
* MAKE MONO: convert sample from stereo to mono. Choose whether to keep the Left channel, the Right channel or mix Left + Right.
* BOUNCE: save selected area to a new pad
* EXPORT: export the sample, or all selected samples, as audio files. [Read more](../7-main-menu#77-export-options)
* CONVERT TO CHOPPER / CONVERT TO SAMPLE [SAMURAI]: turn the pad into a Chopper instrument and back. [Read more](#418-chopper-samurai)
