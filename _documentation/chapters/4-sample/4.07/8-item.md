Tap the arrow button in the top-right corner of the sample editor to open the tools menu:  
<span lang="zh-CN">点击采样编辑器右上角的箭头，打开工具菜单。</span>
* LABEL: add a custom label to the pad  
  <span lang="zh-CN">LABEL（标签）：给打击垫添加自定义名称。</span>
* INFO: show details about the sample. [Read more](#414-sample-info)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">INFO（信息）：查看采样详情。[了解更多](#414-sample-info)。</span>
* EDIT: open the wave editor. [Read more](#416-wave-editor)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">EDIT（编辑）：打开波形编辑器。[了解更多](#416-wave-editor)。</span>
* COLOR: choose a colour for the pad. [Read more](#415-pad-and-sequence-colours)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">COLOR（颜色）：为打击垫选择颜色。[了解更多](#415-pad-and-sequence-colours)。</span>
* CROP: trim the sample to selected area  
  <span lang="zh-CN">CROP（裁剪）：只保留采样的选区。</span>
* NORMALIZE: normalizes the sample to 0 dB  
  <span lang="zh-CN">NORMALIZE（归一化）：将采样的峰值电平归一化到 0 dB。</span>
* AUTO-CHOP [SAMURAI]: divide sample into chops. [Read more](../10-in-app-purchases#101-samurai) about SAMURAI add-on  
  <span lang="zh-CN">AUTO-CHOP（自动切片）[SAMURAI]：将采样切成多个片段。[了解 SAMURAI 扩展](../10-in-app-purchases#101-samurai)。</span>
* SPLIT STEMS: separate stems into drums, vocals, bass and other instruments. [Read more](../4-sample#410-split-stems)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">SPLIT STEMS（分离音轨）：分离鼓、人声、贝斯和其他乐器。[了解更多](../4-sample#410-split-stems)。</span>
* MAKE MONO: convert sample from stereo to mono. Choose whether to keep the Left channel, the Right channel or mix Left + Right.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MAKE MONO（转为单声道）：将立体声采样转换为单声道，可保留左声道、右声道，或将两者混合。</span>
* BOUNCE: save selected area to a new pad  
  <span lang="zh-CN">BOUNCE（混缩）：将选区保存到一个新的打击垫。</span>
* EXPORT: export the sample, or all selected samples, as audio files. [Read more](../7-main-menu#77-export-options)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">EXPORT（导出）：把当前采样或所有选中的采样导出为音频文件。[查看导出选项](../7-main-menu#77-export-options)。</span>
* CONVERT TO CHOPPER / CONVERT TO SAMPLE [SAMURAI]: turn the pad into a Chopper instrument and back. [Read more](#418-chopper-samurai)  
  <span lang="zh-CN">CONVERT TO CHOPPER／CONVERT TO SAMPLE（转为切片乐器／转为采样）[SAMURAI]：在 Chopper 乐器和采样打击垫之间转换。[了解更多](#418-chopper-samurai)。</span>
