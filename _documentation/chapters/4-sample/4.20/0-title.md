## 4.20. Plugin Instruments
Koala can host third party plugins: AUv3 on iOS, Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, CLAP and VST3 on Windows. On iOS this is part of the MIXER in-app purchase. On macOS and Windows it is built in, nothing to buy or enable. Instrument plugins live on pads, effect plugins go in mixer slots. [Read more](../6-perform#66-plugin-effects) about plugin effects.
