Open the SAMPLES browser and go to MORE. After Koala has scanned your plugins you will see a section per plugin format your platform supports (AUDIO UNITS, VST3, CLAP). Sort them A-Z or by COMPANY. Tap a plugin and load it onto a free pad.  
<span lang="zh-CN">打开 SAMPLES（采样库），进入 MORE（更多）。Koala 扫描插件后，会按当前平台支持的格式显示栏目（AUDIO UNITS、VST3、CLAP）。可按字母顺序（A-Z）或厂商（COMPANY）排序。点击插件并将其载入空打击垫。</span>

Tap EDIT with the pad selected. OPEN EDITOR shows the plugin's own interface, CONTROLS shows Koala's generic knobs for the plugin's parameters, which can be MIDI mapped and automated like any other control.  
<span lang="zh-CN">选中打击垫后点击 EDIT（编辑）。OPEN EDITOR（打开编辑器）会打开插件自身的界面。CONTROLS（控件）显示 Koala 为插件参数提供的通用旋钮。这些旋钮和其他控件一样，支持 MIDI 映射和自动化。</span>

Set the input source to RESAMPLE FROM APP to record the plugin's output onto a sample pad.  
<span lang="zh-CN">将输入源设为 RESAMPLE FROM APP（从应用内重采样），可把插件的输出录到采样打击垫。</span>

Note: hosting third-party plugins inside Koala is only available in the standalone app, not when Koala itself runs as a plugin inside a DAW. Plugins that fail Koala's safety scan are greyed out and cannot be loaded. On macOS, enable "Hide Apple Audio Units" in the EXTRAS settings to hide the system Audio Units from the list.  
<span lang="zh-CN">注意：只有独立应用可以加载第三方插件。Koala 本身作为 DAW 插件运行时无法使用此功能。未通过 Koala 安全扫描的插件会变灰，无法加载。在 macOS 上，可在 EXTRAS（其他）设置中开启 Hide Apple Audio Units（隐藏 Apple Audio Units），从列表中隐藏系统自带的 Audio Units。</span>
