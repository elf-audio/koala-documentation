Open the SAMPLES browser and go to MORE. After Koala has scanned your plugins you will see a section per plugin format your platform supports (AUDIO UNITS, VST3, CLAP). Sort them A-Z or by COMPANY. Tap a plugin and load it onto a free pad.

Tap EDIT with the pad selected. OPEN EDITOR shows the plugin's own interface, CONTROLS shows Koala's generic knobs for the plugin's parameters, which can be MIDI mapped and automated like any other control.

Set the input source to RESAMPLE FROM APP to record the plugin's output onto a sample pad.

Note: plugin hosting is only available in the standalone app, not when Koala itself runs as an AUv3. Plugins that fail Koala's safety scan are greyed out and cannot be loaded. On macOS, enable "Hide Apple Audio Units" in the EXTRAS settings to hide the system Audio Units from the list.
