The info window shows the sample's name and file name, format and bit depth, sample rate, channels (mono or stereo), length in seconds and in bars at the current tempo, file size, where it came from (recorded, imported, resampled, stem split or sample pack) and when it was created.

If the sample has a tempo, either tagged by the sample pack or detected by Koala, tap SET BPM TO to set your song's tempo to match it. Samples from packs may also show a key, root note and tags.

Pro tip: on desktop, press Cmd/Ctrl+I to open sample info for the selected pad.
