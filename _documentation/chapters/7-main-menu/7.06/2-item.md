Tap any export option and the EXPORT window appears, where you choose the format before Koala renders the files. [Read more](#77-export-options) about the choices in that window.

Sample export options give you a choice of RAW and EDITED samples. RAW exports your samples exactly as they were recorded or imported. EDITED exports them with your edits applied: the selection area, pitch, volume, pan, envelope, reverse and so on.
