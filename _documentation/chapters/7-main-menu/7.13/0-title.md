<h2 id="713-song-options">7.13. Song Options<br><span lang="zh-CN">7.13. 工程选项</span></h2>
The top of the MENU shows the current song: its name, size, available storage and RAM, and whether it lives in iCloud. Tap the cog next to the song name for song related options.  
<span lang="zh-CN">MENU 顶部会显示当前工程的名称和大小、设备可用存储空间和运行内存，以及工程是否存放在 iCloud。点击工程名称旁的齿轮，打开工程选项。</span>
