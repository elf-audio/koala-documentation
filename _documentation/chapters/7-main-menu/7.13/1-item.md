* DELETE UNUSED PADS: removes every pad that is not played by any sequence. Koala tells you how many pads it is about to delete and asks for confirmation. Great for slimming down a finished song.
* CROP ALL SAMPLES: trims every sample to its selected area, throwing away the audio outside the start and end markers. This can shrink a song considerably.
* EXPORT SONG: share the song as a .koala file, same as SHARE KOALA SONG in the export menu.
* SET SONG BG: choose a background image for this song only. [Read more](../8-settings#84-extras-settings) about background images and effects.

Pro tip: in the EXTRAS settings you can save the current song as a template with SAVE TEMPLATE. NEW then starts from that template instead of an empty song. REMOVE TEMPLATE goes back to the standard empty song.
