## 7.2. Load Song

{% flexible_image link="menu-load-song.png" alt="Load song screen" %}

Tap MENU, then LOAD to bring up the song list. Tap on any song in the list to load it. Songs show a small preview of their pads, and you can tap the play icon to hear a short preview before loading.

* Pull the list down to reveal the search field and type to filter your songs by name.
* Tap the Sort dropdown to order songs A-Z, Z-A, by File Size or by Recent.
* On iOS and macOS with iCloud Drive enabled, switch between DEVICE and ICLOUD to see songs stored locally or in your iCloud Drive.
* Swipe a song to the left to SHARE it or DELETE it. Koala asks before deleting. Choose DELETE (DON'T ASK AGAIN) to skip the question in future.
* Tap IMPORT SONG to add a .koala file from a different location, i.e. a cloud drive or the Downloads folder. The song is copied into your songs folder.
