<h2 id="712-get-live-lite">7.12. Get Live Lite<br><span lang="zh-CN">7.12. 获取 Live Lite</span></h2>

GET LIVE LITE is shown on iOS. Tap it to open a window with an email entry field. Confirm your email and you will receive a free version of Ableton Live Lite.  
<span lang="zh-CN">iOS 上会显示 GET LIVE LITE（获取 Live Lite）。点击后打开电子邮件地址输入窗口。确认地址后，即可获得免费的 Ableton Live Lite。</span>
