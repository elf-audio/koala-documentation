Select IMPORT AUDIO to open the file browser. Last used destination is the default starting screen. Depending on your OS, wide range of audio formats is available for import.  
<span lang="zh-CN">选择 IMPORT AUDIO（导入音频）打开文件浏览器，默认显示上次使用的位置。可导入的音频格式取决于操作系统。</span>

Generally speaking, anything you are able to preview and listen to on your device is available for import.  
<span lang="zh-CN">一般来说，设备上能预览和播放的音频都可以导入。</span>

Which file picker opens is set by "Import Audio" in the GENERAL settings: Koala's own browser, the SYSTEM picker, or AudioShare on iOS.  
<span lang="zh-CN">GENERAL（常规）设置中的 Import Audio 选项决定使用哪个文件选择器：Koala 自带浏览器、SYSTEM（系统选择器）或 iOS 上的 AudioShare。</span>

Other things you can import:  
<span lang="zh-CN">还可以导入以下内容。</span>
* MIDI files (.mid): drop one onto a sequence slot, or open it from the browser, and the notes are written into a sequence. Each MIDI note maps to a pad in order, starting with bank A pad 1.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">MIDI 文件（.mid）：将文件拖到音序槽位，或从浏览器中打开，即可把音符写入音序。MIDI 音符按顺序映射到打击垫，从 A 组第 1 个打击垫开始。</span>
* Koala songs (.koala) and sample packs (.kpk): opening them from another app or the Files app imports them into Koala.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">Koala 工程（.koala）和采样包（.kpk）：从其他应用或「文件」App 打开，即可导入 Koala。</span>
* MIDI maps and QWERTY maps (.json) exported from the EXTRAS settings.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">从 EXTRAS（其他）设置导出的 MIDI 映射和 QWERTY 键盘映射（.json）。</span>
* On macOS and Windows, drag audio files from Finder/Explorer or your DAW onto pads, including into the Koala DAW plugin. Drag pads out of Koala onto the desktop/Explorer or straight into your DAW.  
  <span lang="zh-CN">在 macOS 和 Windows 上，可将音频从访达、文件资源管理器或 DAW 拖到打击垫，包括 Koala DAW 插件中的打击垫。也可以将 Koala 打击垫拖到桌面、文件资源管理器或直接拖入 DAW。</span>

Audio files with more than two channels are automatically mixed down to stereo on import.  
<span lang="zh-CN">超过两个声道的音频文件在导入时会自动缩混为立体声。</span>
