Koala can hold up to 64 samples of any length. 32 sequences are available for arrangement. 16 multitouch live effects can be used during the performance and over 30 effects can be assigned to 5 channels in the mixer section. Pads can also hold instruments: the Quokka synth, the Chopper, MIDI out and third party plugins.
