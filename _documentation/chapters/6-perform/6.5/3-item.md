Open an effect and tap the hamburger menu for more options:
* DELETE: remove the effect from the slot.
* REPLACE: swap it for a different effect, keeping the slot.
* INFO: a short explanation of what the effect does.
* **Presets**: save, load, import and export presets for mixer bus effects.
* Bypass: the power button next to the title switches the effect off without removing it, handy for A/B comparisons. Tapping a bypassed effect's controls asks whether you want to switch it back on.

Pro tip: you can also hold and drag an effect slot up into the trash area above the mixer channels to remove it.
