## 6.6. Plugin Effects
Mixer slots can hold third party effect plugins as well as Koala's own effects: AUv3 on iOS, Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, CLAP and VST3 on Windows. On iOS this is part of the MIXER in-app purchase. On macOS and Windows it is built in, nothing to buy or enable.
