## 6.6. Plugin Effects
Mixer slots can hold third party effect plugins as well as Koala's own effects: AUv3 on iOS, Audio Units, CLAP and VST3 on macOS, CLAP and VST3 on Windows and Linux. On iOS this is part of the MIXER in-app purchase. On macOS, Windows and Linux it is built into the standalone app, nothing to buy or enable.
