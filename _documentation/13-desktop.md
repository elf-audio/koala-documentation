  Koala Sampler on macOS, Windows and Linux: the free standalone app with SAMURAI and MIXER, and the Koala Plugin Bundle (AU / VST3 / CLAP and Koala FX) for desktop DAWs.
---
Koala runs as a free standalone app on macOS, Windows and Linux, with SAMURAI and MIXER built in. Everything marked [SAMURAI] or [MIXER] in this manual is available without buying or enabling anything. The desktop app is the same Koala as on your phone, with a few extras for mouse, keyboard and files.

The paid [Koala Plugin Bundle](#134-koala-plugin-bundle) brings Koala and Koala FX into your desktop DAW: AU / VST3 on macOS, VST3 on Windows, and CLAP / VST3 on Linux.
