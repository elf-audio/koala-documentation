Koala runs as a standalone app on macOS, Windows and Linux. The desktop version is the same Koala as on your phone, with a few extras for mouse, keyboard and files. The SAMURAI and MIXER features are built into the desktop versions, so everything marked [SAMURAI] or [MIXER] in this manual is available without buying or enabling anything.
